# Figures

- `study_workflow.png` — chronological decision and evaluation workflow.
- `s4_coverage_budget.png` — mean failure coverage by budget for all evaluated
  strategies.
- `s4_lift_budget.png` — mean lift by budget for all evaluated strategies.

All result charts are generated from `../tables/s4_results.csv` by
`../scripts/build_figures.py`. They aggregate the four decision dates consistently
with the manuscript tables. S5 SMART/ML diagnostic scores are intentionally excluded.
